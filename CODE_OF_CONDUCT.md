# Code of conduct

G3 Tech projects are collaborative spaces. Be respectful, constructive, and considerate of differences in experience, identity, and perspective.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable. Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to an organization owner through GitHub. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with attention to the safety of everyone involved.

This policy follows the principles of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
